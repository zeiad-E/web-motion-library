# The idea engine: 20–100+ strong, varied ideas

## 1. Start from the brief

Write down before generating:
- **Subject nouns**: the brand's real objects (tooth, key, deed, cup, chip, shoe, scales, map pin, the founder's face, the product).
- **Verbs of the business**: what it *does* (cleans, protects, builds, delivers, heals, connects, answers).
- **Moment of truth**: the before → after the customer pays for.
- **Audience mood** from `audience.md`.

## 2. Combine across four axes

Each idea = **Trigger × Subject × Transformation × Narrative**. Rotate through every axis so ideas don't repeat.

| Trigger | Transformation | Narrative arc |
|---|---|---|
| Page load / time | Assemble from parts | Creation (nothing → object) |
| Scroll position | Explode into layers | Journey (A → B along a path) |
| Scroll velocity | Morph shape → shape | Reveal (hidden → seen) |
| Hover / cursor position | Fly through / zoom into | Transformation (before → after) |
| Click / tap | Draw / trace a line | Inside look (surface → how it works) |
| Drag / swipe | Pour / fill / grow | Choice (visitor picks a path) |
| Idle (no input) | Rotate to a new angle | Countdown / progress |
| Device tilt (phones) | Scatter / gather particles | Conversation (question → answer) |
| Section enter/leave | Mask / unmask (lens, wipe) | Loop (end returns to start) |
| Form success | Bend / liquefy / ripple | Proof (claim → evidence) |

Example combinations:
- *Scroll × tooth × assemble × creation* → shards build a tooth as you scroll.
- *Cursor × X-ray × mask × reveal* → a lens shows hidden cavities.
- *Drag × before/after × clip × transformation* → whitening slider.
- *Load × keys × assemble × creation* (real estate) → a house key forms from brass fragments.
- *Scroll × deed × unfold × inside look* (legal) → a contract unfolds into the steps of the case.
- *Load × tangled line × untangle × transformation* (legal, anxious clients) → a knot loosens into a calm line that becomes the page's thread.
- *Scroll × coffee × pour × creation* (café) → the cup fills as you read the menu.
- *Drag × room photo × relight × before/after* (interior design) → day/night or empty/furnished slider.
- *Hover × chip × explode × inside look* (hardware/SaaS) → the product splits into its layers.
- *Scroll × parcel × travel × journey* (logistics) → a box rides a map route; milestones are delivery steps.
- *Idle × mascot × turn head × conversation* (kids/education) → the mascot looks at whatever you hover.

## 3. Cover every family

When asked for N ideas, spread them across all 12 families in `catalog.md` (scroll, 3D/WebGL, media, typography, cursor, navigation, loaders, aesthetic, micro-interactions, data, sound, AI assets). Rough split for 100 ideas: 12 scroll, 12 3D/WebGL, 10 media, 10 typography, 10 cursor/hover, 6 navigation, 5 entrance, 8 aesthetic, 12 micro-interactions, 6 data/explanation, 2 sound/haptics, 7 concept-level "big ideas" (whole-site concepts).

Scale it down for small requests: for ~25 ideas, aim for 3 scroll, 2 3D/WebGL or one signature object, 2 media, 3 typography, 2 cursor/hover (with touch versions), 2 navigation/entrance, 2 aesthetic, 4 micro-interactions, 2 data/explanation, 3 brand-specific big ideas. Under 10 ideas: concept + signature + 5 supporting.

At least 10% must be **brand-specific signature ideas** that only this client could use (built from their subject nouns). At least 20% must be **cheap wins** (L/L cost) that still feel premium.

## 4. Push past the obvious

For each safe idea, write its bolder sibling:
- Fade-up headline → headline letters assemble from the same shards as the hero object.
- Logo marquee → marquee whose speed and lean follow your scroll.
- Testimonial slider → swipe deck you can throw.
- Timeline → path that draws itself while the hero object rides it as the marker.
- FAQ accordion → each answer opens with the object turning to "look" at it.

## 5. Score

| Score | Question |
|---|---|
| Impact 1–5 | Will people remember or share it? Does it create agency, curiosity or reward? |
| Fit 1–5 | Does it suit this audience's mood and the brand's meaning? |
| Effort 1–5 | Build time and asset cost (5 = very hard) |
| Weight 1–5 | Runtime cost on a mid-range phone (5 = heavy) |

Priority ≈ Impact × Fit ÷ (Effort + Weight). Ship: 1 signature (high impact even if heavy), 3–6 supporting, micro-interactions throughout.

## 6. Output format for idea lists

Use a numbered list grouped by family, each line:

`N. **Name** — what the visitor sees. *Built with:* technique/tool. *Why it works here:* audience effect. *Cost:* effort/weight (L/M/H).`

Then always finish with:
1. **The concept** (one sentence) and **the thread** (what travels through the page).
2. **Recommended set**: signature + supporting effects, in page order, with how each hands off to the next (see `choreography.md`).
3. **Risks**: phones, accessibility, RTL, assets needed.
